# EduBook Presentation Script

This script divides the 15 slides equally between Neil and Elyas so that both speak for the exact same amount of time, as requested by the teacher's rubric. 
The English used is natural, easy to pronounce (B2 level), and includes the mandatory phrases.

---

### Slide 1: Title
**Neil:** 
"Hello everyone, good morning. Today, my partner Elyas and I are going to present our project: EduBook, the University Events Marketplace."

### Slide 2: Introduction to the Project
**Elyas:** 
"First, let us introduce ourselves. I am Elyas Iqbal, and he is Neil Andrei Sarinas. We are both studying DAW (Web Application Development) here at STUCOM."

### Slide 3: What's the project about?
**Neil:** 
"So, what is EduBook? Basically, it is a web platform that brings all university events together in one single place. It connects universities with students who are looking for talks, workshops, and academic events in Spain."

### Slide 4: Why was the project done?
**Elyas:** 
"Why did we create this? The main problem right now is that universities post their events on many different websites and social media. Because the information is everywhere, it is very hard for students to find good events and keep track of them."

### Slide 5: Our Motivations
**Neil:** 
"Our motivation is simple. We believe that finding a good academic path should be easy, not stressful. We want to make education accessible for everyone, helping students discover their true vocation."

### Slide 6: Goals & Objectives
**Elyas:** 
"Our main goals are to centralize all events on one website and connect universities with students. To do this, our objectives are to simplify the registration process for students, and empower universities with a dashboard to manage their events."

### Slide 7: The Company
**Neil:** 
"As a company, our mission is to remove the barriers that stop students from finding educational opportunities. Our vision is to become the number one platform for educational events in Spain. And our values are Accessibility, Innovation, and building a great Community."

### Slide 8: Website Tour (Live Demo)
**Elyas:** 
"Now, let's see it in action. Here we have a live demo of our website..." 
*(Elyas clicks the button 'Start Website Tour' and briefly shows the page)* 
"...As you can see, the interface is completely interactive, clean, and ready to use."

### Slide 9: Market Analysis
**Neil:** 
"Before building EduBook, we took the best ideas from famous platforms. We looked at Eventbrite for professional event management, Meetup for its strong community focus, and Booking.com for its easy-to-use filters."

### Slide 10: Technical Explanation (I)
**Elyas:** 
"Moving on to the technical part. **In order to** develop the front-end, we worked on the structure of our page carefully. **Firstly**, Figma, **which is** a designing tool similar to Wix, **was used to** design the first prototype."

### Slide 11: Technical Explanation (II)
**Neil:** 
"**Afterwards**, coding **was written by means of** using Visual Studio Code. For the front-end we used HTML5, Vanilla CSS, and JavaScript. For the back-end, we used PHP following an MVC architecture, and local JSON files for saving data."

### Slide 12: Advanced Features (III)
**Elyas:** 
"But we also added extra technologies. Instead of a normal database, we used a modern Serverless system. We used **n8n Webhooks** and **Supabase** to do the Login and Register securely. We also added an AI Chatbot on our website that can answer questions."

### Slide 13: Conclusion to the Project
**Neil:** 
"**Overall, we conclude that** EduBook is a great solution for the university events problem. We successfully built a working website with advanced tools like n8n and AI, and we believe this platform will make life much easier for students."

### Slide 14: Teamwork & Difficulties
**Elyas:** 
"How did we work as a team? We communicated every day. We divided the tasks: one focused more on the design and the other on the PHP logic. Our biggest challenge was connecting PHP sessions with n8n and JSON files, but we learned a lot and made it work."

### Slide 15: Suggestions for the future
**Neil:** 
"Finally, for future teamwork, we suggest planning our time better using agile methods like Scrum, and testing the code together more often to avoid bugs."

**Elyas:** 
"We also suggest learning more about new tools like n8n before starting to code directly. Thank you very much for your attention."

**Neil:** 
"Do you have any questions?"
